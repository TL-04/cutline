# Cutline

A video editor that runs entirely in your browser. Your files never leave your computer.

**Open it:** https://tl-04.github.io/cutline/

- Cut, split, trim and arrange video and audio clips on a timeline
- Separate audio from video; multi-track OBS (.mkv) recordings import every audio track
- Auto subtitles (speech recognition runs locally), with styling, colours and sync to audio
- Export as WebM, horizontal 16:9 or vertical 9:16 (crop or blurred background)

## Install as an app

In Chrome or Edge, open the link above and click the **Install** icon at the right of the address bar
(or menu → *Cast, save and share* → *Install page as app*). Cutline then opens in its own window and works offline.
The speech model (~150 MB) downloads the first time you generate subtitles.

Works best in Chrome or Edge. The whole app is the single file `index.html`.
